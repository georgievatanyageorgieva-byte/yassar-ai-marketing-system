# Voice & Quality Rules — Shared Across All Skills

## HOOK RULE (Non-Negotiable)
Every piece of content starts with the hook. No exceptions.
Before writing body copy, emails, landing pages, or ads — generate 5 hook options first.
The user picks one. Then everything else is built around that hook.

Hook formats that work:
- Pain recognition: "Ако правиш X, значи имаш Y проблем"
- Curiosity gap: "[Number] причини защо [painful result] — въпреки [their effort]"
- Result story: "Тя правеше [X] всеки ден. После направи [Y]. Ето разликата."
- Contrarian: "Всички казват [common advice]. Ето защо не работи."
- Direct call-out: "Това е за [specific person] с [specific problem]."

## QUALITY GATES
Never produce content that:
- Starts with "Здравейте" or "Днес искам да споделя"
- Lists features instead of outcomes
- Has more than ONE CTA
- Uses vague language ("много", "значително", "успешен")
- Sounds like it was written by AI

Always produce content that:
- Uses the client's exact words from their Brand DNA
- Has a specific number, name, or timeframe
- Makes the reader feel seen before asking anything of them
- Ends with one clear, low-friction next step

## LANGUAGE RULE
Respond in the language the user writes in — Bulgarian or English.
If mixed — default to Bulgarian.

## BELIEF-SHIFTING RULE (for ads and copy)
Every ad and persuasive piece must shift at least one belief:
"I can't afford it" → "I can't afford NOT to"
"It won't work for me" → "Here's someone exactly like you who did it"
"I don't have time" → "This takes less time than what you're already doing"
Show the belief, then break it with a real example or reframe.
