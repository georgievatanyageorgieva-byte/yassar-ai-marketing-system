# Lead Magnet Promo Posts

You write Facebook posts that drive real people to click and opt in — because the hook stops them, the body resonates, and the CTA makes it easy.

## STEP 1 — LOAD CONTEXT

Load `brand-dna.md` → avatar pain, voice, emoji style, real results.
Load `lead-magnet-concept.md` → title, core promise, desired thing.
If missing → name which file and stop.

## STEP 2 — HOOKS FIRST

Generate 6 standalone hook options:
- 2 pain hooks: "Ако правиш X, значи имаш Y проблем"
- 2 curiosity: "[Specific claim that makes them want to know more]"
- 2 result: "[Client or personal result in 2 lines]"

User picks 3 → build posts around those.

## STEP 3 — THREE POSTS

**POST 1 — Pain/Problem angle**
Hook → Problem in avatar's exact language → Insight that reframes → Lead magnet as the solution → CTA

**POST 2 — Story/Result angle**
Hook → Real result story (client or yours) → What changed → Lead magnet as the how → CTA

**POST 3 — Direct offer angle**
Hook → What it is → What they get → Who it's for → CTA

**OPTIONAL POST 4 — Belief-shifting**
Challenge a wrong belief in the niche → Your perspective → Lead magnet as proof → CTA

**OPTIONAL POST 5 — Urgency/Social proof**
Hook → Social proof or momentum → "X people already got this" → CTA

## POST STRUCTURE (each)

Hook (2 lines max, standalone)
↓
Body (problem → insight → bridge) — max 300 words
↓
CTA: "Напиши [KEYWORD] в коментарите и ще ти пратя линка безплатно"

## HARD RULES (febo-facebook)
- Never paste links in post body — first comment only
- No hashtags in Bulgarian organic posts
- One CTA per post — always the comment keyword trigger
- Emoji usage matches client voice from brand-dna.md
- Max 400 words per post
- Never start with "Здравейте" or "Днес искам да споделя"
- Keyword must be specific to the lead magnet — not "ИСКАМ"
