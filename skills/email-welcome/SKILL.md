# Welcome Email Sequence

You write the evergreen welcome sequence — the first thing a new subscriber experiences when they join the list. These 5 emails run automatically over 7 days and set the tone for the entire relationship.

**Goal:** Trust → Authority → Desire → Offer

---

## STEP 1 — LOAD CONTEXT

Load `brand-dna.md` → extract:
- Coach name + voice
- Avatar (who they are, their pain, their desire)
- Transformation story (before / epiphany / after)
- The mechanism (what makes the approach unique)
- Main offer (name + price range + outcome)
- Top 2-3 proof points (results, numbers)

If not found → stop and ask for the path.

---

## STEP 2 — CLARIFY ENTRY POINT

Ask:
> "Откъде влизат тези нови абонати?
> [A] Лийд магнит — изтеглили са безплатен ресурс
> [B] Opt-in страница — записали са се директно за бюлетина
> [C] Challenge регистрация (free) — вече са в challenge-а
> [D] Нещо друго — кажи ми"

This determines the tone of Email 1.

---

## STEP 3 — CONFIRM OFFER TYPE

Ask:
> "Каква е основната оферта, към която води тази последователност?
> (Програма, интензив, менторство, discovery call — и приблизителна цена)
> Ако нямаш готова оферта в момента, напиши 'без оферта' — ще приключим с полезно съдържание."

---

## STEP 4 — BUILD SEQUENCE

Build all 5 emails one by one. After each, ask:
> "A) Продължи / B) Промени нещо: [кажи какво]"

---

### EMAIL 1 — ДОБРЕ ДОШЪЛ (изпраща се веднага)

**Purpose:** Confirm subscription, set expectations, deliver on promise (if lead magnet).

**Structure:**
- Subject: 3 options (curiosity / benefit / direct — no emojis)
- Opening: Acknowledge what they did (downloaded X / subscribed to Y)
- Body: 
  - Deliver what was promised (link to lead magnet if applicable)
  - Who you are in 2 sentences (not a bio — one result you've achieved)
  - What they can expect from you (content type + frequency)
  - One question to activate replies ("Кажи ми — какво е най-голямото ти предизвикателство с [PAIN AREA] в момента?")
- CTA: Reply with their answer OR click to resource
- Length: 150-200 words

**Tone:** Warm, direct, zero fluff. Like a message from a person, not a brand.

---

### EMAIL 2 — ИСТОРИЯТА (изпраща се на ден 2)

**Purpose:** Build relatability through your before/epiphany/after story.

**Structure:**
- Subject: 3 options (curiosity hook about a failure or turning point)
- Opening: Start IN the story — not "Днес ще ти разкажа историята си"
- Body:
  - The BEFORE: where you were (be specific — numbers, emotions, the low point)
  - The EPIPHANY: what you discovered that changed everything (the mechanism)
  - The AFTER: what's possible now — for you AND for them
  - Bridge: "Ето защо вярвам, че [AVATAR] може да [OUTCOME] — дори без [OBJECTION]"
- CTA: Reply with "Да, разпознавам се" or a soft resource link
- Length: 250-350 words

**Tone:** Vulnerable but not weak. Honest. The story should make them think "That's me."

---

### EMAIL 3 — СТОЙНОСТТА (изпраща се на ден 4)

**Purpose:** Teach something genuinely useful. Build authority without selling.

**Structure:**
- Subject: 3 options (specific insight or counterintuitive claim)
- Opening: One sentence — the most important thing they need to know about [TOPIC]
- Body:
  - The framework / insight / mistake they're probably making
  - Why it matters (consequence of doing it wrong)
  - The correct approach (brief — 3 bullets max)
  - How this connects to your mechanism
- CTA: "Ако искаш да разбереш как прилагаме това на практика, отговори с 'ИСКАМ' и ще ти изпратя нещо."
- Length: 200-300 words

**Tone:** Teacher mode. Confident. No hedging. One idea, done well.

---

### EMAIL 4 — ДОКАЗАТЕЛСТВО (изпраща се на ден 6)

**Purpose:** Social proof — show that it works for people like them.

**Structure:**
- Subject: 3 options (result-focused — specific numbers or timeframe)
- Opening: "Нека ти разкажа за [CLIENT NAME / 'един от клиентите ми']..."
- Body:
  - Before: where they were (match avatar's pain)
  - What changed: what they did with you / your system
  - After: specific result (numbers + timeframe)
  - Quote from them (if available)
  - Bridge: "Ти не трябва да бъдеш перфектен. Трябва да имаш правилната система."
- CTA: Soft mention of the offer — "Ако искаш да видиш как работи, [LINK]" or "Отговори с 'РАЗКАЖИ МИ' и ще те свържа с повече информация"
- Length: 200-300 words

**Tone:** Storytelling. Let the client's result speak. Don't over-sell.

---

### EMAIL 5 — ОФЕРТАТА (изпраща се на ден 7-8)

**Purpose:** Direct, confident offer presentation.

**Structure:**
- Subject: 3 options (direct / benefit / urgency — no tricks)
- Opening: Acknowledge the journey — "Миналата седмица ти разказах за [X]. Днес искам да ти кажа нещо директно."
- Body:
  - What you offer (name + 3-sentence description)
  - Who it's for (YES list — 4-5 bullets)
  - What they get (3-4 key outcomes — NOT a feature list)
  - The investment (price or price range)
  - How to take the next step (one clear CTA: booking link / reply / application)
  - Guarantee or risk-reversal (if applicable)
- P.S.: Handles the #1 objection ("Ако се чудиш дали е за теб...")
- Length: 300-400 words

**Tone:** Confident. Not pushy. Like a trusted advisor, not a salesperson.

---

## EMAIL RULES (apply to all 5)

- No emojis in subject lines (spam risk)
- Facebook posts and body text: emojis OK
- Every email has ONE CTA — not two, not three
- Under 400 words per email (except Email 5 which can go to 400)
- Bulgarian only
- Write FROM the coach's voice (extracted from brand-dna.md)
- Never use "Скъпи [Ime]" — use "[Ime]," or just start with the hook
- No "Надявам се имейлът ти намери добре" — get to the point immediately
